About GNDU and this guide

A place to learn, connect, and grow.

Established on 24 November 1969, Guru Nanak Dev University (GNDU) is a residential and affiliating university in Amritsar. Its academic work spans the humanities, sciences, professional disciplines, and technology, with teaching and research across its academic units.

Campus resources described by GNDU include Bhai Gurdas Library, sports facilities, health services, hostels, and student support. Student-welfare activities include cultural programmes, youth festivals, sports, clubs, seminars, and workshops; schedules and access vary, so check official university notices for current details. This independent guide is not the official GNDU website.
